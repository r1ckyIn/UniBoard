    - `name: Frontend CI`
    - Triggers: `push` to `branches: [main]` with `paths: ['frontend/**', '.github/workflows/frontend-ci.yml']`; `pull_request` with `paths: ['frontend/**']`
    - Single job `check` on `ubuntu-latest` with `defaults.run.working-directory: frontend`:
      1. `actions/checkout@v4`
      2. `pnpm/action-setup@v4` (no version needed -- reads from packageManager field or auto-detects)
      3. `actions/setup-node@v4` with `node-version: 22` and `cache: 'pnpm'` and `cache-dependency-path: frontend/pnpm-lock.yaml`
      4. `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile`
      5. Step "Lint (ESLint)": `pnpm lint`
      6. Step "Type check (TypeScript)": `pnpm typecheck`
      7. Step "Build": `pnpm build`

    Both workflows use `on.push` and `on.pull_request` triggers with path filters to avoid unnecessary CI runs when unrelated files change.
