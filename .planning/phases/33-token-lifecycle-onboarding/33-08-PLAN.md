```markdown
### §7.X Email Confirmation Policy

**Status:** Permanently OFF in production (Supabase Studio toggle).

**Rationale:** USYD students use `@uni.sydney.edu.au` mailboxes governed by Mimecast Secure Email Gateway. Mimecast quarantines messages from the `uniboard.uk` sender domain (Held Messages) and releases them only via a digest sent every 3 hours. This makes any signup-time confirmation flow untenable — users would wait 0–3 hours for the confirmation email to surface, then click a link that may have expired in the meantime.

**Mitigation strategy (Phase 33 AUTH-HARDEN):**
1. **Primary auth path:** Google OAuth via USYD Google Workspace, bypassing email entirely (AUTH-HARDEN-01).
2. **Email-based password reset** still available for users who prefer email-based credentials, with a "Resend email" button (60s cooldown) to recover from quarantine delays (AUTH-HARDEN-03).
3. **USYD-specific banner** on the registration page warning users about Mimecast Held Messages and recommending Google sign-in (AUTH-HARDEN-02).

**Recipient deliverability** (inbox vs Junk vs Mimecast quarantine) is a recipient-policy issue, NOT a sender-side acceptance criterion. Resend dashboard reports 100% Delivered for non-bounced addresses; SPF/DKIM/DMARC all pass.

See §16.X for deployment configuration.
```
