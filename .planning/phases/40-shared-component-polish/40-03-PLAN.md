**HIGH-RISK DEPENDENCY (RESEARCH Assumption A6 + Open Question 1)**:

D-40-08's `translateX(-156px)` → `translateX(0)` geometry has interpretation ambiguity per RESEARCH Q6 (lines 1326-1496). The literal reading shows inner's RIGHTMOST 68px visible by default, but conventional sidebar UX (Linear, Notion, ChatGPT) shows ICONS at the LEFT edge in collapsed mode.

RESEARCH Pattern 5 (lines 791-887) implements the literal D-40-08 reading: outer `overflow-hidden`, inner `absolute left:0 w:224px translateX(-156px) → translateX(0)`. This means:
- Default state: inner shifted LEFT 156px → only inner's [156..224] range = its rightmost 68px is visible inside outer's clipping window.
- Hover state: inner at translateX(0) → inner's [0..68] = its leftmost 68px is visible. Inner's [68..224] = the right 156px extends BEYOND outer's clipping window (overflow-hidden clips it).

This means **on hover, the user sees inner's LEFT 68px** (icons + start of labels). Since inner has overflow-hidden via outer, the right 156px of inner is clipped — **labels appear to slide IN from the right** as the inner translates from -156px → 0.

Actually this DOES work conventionally if the layout is: icons at LEFT edge of inner panel (x ≈ 14-34 in inner's coord space), labels FOLLOW icons (x ≈ 48+). When inner is at translateX(-156px), inner-x=156 lands at outer-x=0; inner-x=224 lands at outer-x=68. So visible in outer = inner's [156..224]. **Icons (at inner-x=14) are NOT visible — they're at outer-x=-142.**

This means the **D-40-08 literal reading hides icons in collapsed state**, which is opposite of v2.0 prototype's "icons visible at left in 68px-wide collapsed sidebar."

RESEARCH explicitly flagged this risk:
> "⚠️ D-40-08's `translateX(-156px)` → `translateX(0)` is **geometrically ambiguous** — implementation may need `overflow:visible` on outer + clip-path on inner OR width-grow trick + transform. Planner must verify against design intent during plan-3 design. Likely the cleanest implementation diverges slightly from D-40-08's literal `translateX` wording."

**RESOLUTION FOR PLAN-3**: A short SPIKE task (Task 1 below) verifies the geometry on a sandbox HTML page BEFORE the structural rewrite. Three candidate geometries:

(a) **Literal D-40-08 (RESEARCH Pattern 5 verbatim)**: outer `overflow-hidden`, inner `translate-x-[-156px] hover:translate-x-0`. Icons at LEFT of inner — NOT visible in collapsed state. Right portion of inner panel visible (right side of nav items, right portion of orange highlight bar). UX: collapsed sidebar shows blank background + right-edge of active highlight.

(b) **Inverted geometry (icons at right of inner, conventional reading reversed)**: outer `overflow-hidden`, inner `translate-x-[-156px] hover:translate-x-0`, but inner content layout is RIGHT-anchored — icons sit at inner-x=190+ (right edge), labels at inner-x=48-180. Then collapsed shows icons (right 68px of inner = the icons area). Hover slides labels in from left.

(c) **Conventional pattern (transform=0 default, overflow:visible on hover)**: outer `overflow-hidden` default → inner natural left-anchored; on hover outer becomes `overflow-visible` AND inner stays at `translateX(0)` BUT z-layer ranks above main content. Inner's right 156px overlays main content as a popover. Conventional UX (icons at left in collapsed, full panel reveals to the right on hover).

The user-facing outcome that matches v2.0 prototype is **(c) conventional pattern**. RESEARCH suggests this is the most likely correct reading despite D-40-08's literal `translateX(-156px)` wording.
