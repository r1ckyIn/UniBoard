How to get your API tokens

Follow these steps to generate tokens from Canvas and Ed. Don’t worry, it’s straightforward!

Canvas LMS
  1. 1
    Go to canvas.sydney.edu.au and log in
  2. 2
    Click your profile picture (bottom-left) → Settings
  3. 3
    Scroll down to Approved Integrations → click + New Access Token
  4. 4
    Enter purpose: UniBoard, leave expiry blank → click Generate Token
  5. 5
    Copy the token immediately — it won’t be shown again!
Ed Discussion
  1. 1
    Go to edstem.org and log in
  2. 2
    Click your avatar (top-right) → Settings
  3. 3
    Navigate to the API Tokens section
  4. 4
    Click Generate Token → name it UniBoard
  5. 5
    Copy the token — you’ll paste it in the next step

Connect your accounts
